    The plan

    Three moves, sequenced so the first one pays for the rest

    1

    Automate intake

    Capture every call and web lead, triage by matter type, run a conflict check, flag time-sensitive deadlines, and book consults — so your admin becomes the exception-handler and nothing slips to voicemail.

    2

    Practice Mission Control

    One secure dashboard for leads, matters, and deadlines on infrastructure the firm owns — fixing the MyCase frustrations by giving you exactly the views you actually use.

    3

    Modernize visibility

    Reclaim and own your analytics and Google profile, update the OVI content to current law, and sharpen the pages searchers and AI answer engines surface — so more of those 200 daily calls are the right calls.

    Why it's built differently

    Automation a law firm can actually trust

    The viral consumer AI agents weren't built for privilege. This is — your data, your keys, full audit trail, human in the loop.

    Off-the-shelf AI agents

    • Reach into your files with broad access
    • Pull untrusted third-party "skills" / code
    • Can act on their own — no audit trail
    • Client data flows through someone else's tool
    • You don't own it and can't prove what it did

    Your owned system

    • Runs on the firm's own infrastructure
    • Row-level isolation & role-based access
    • Every action logged — a complete audit trail
    • Your own AI keys; data stays in your control
    • You own the asset, the data, and the IP
    Where we'd start

    A phased build, starting with the intake win

    Phase 1

    Capture everything

    Stand up automated intake + a clean lead pipeline. Reclaim your analytics and Google profile. Fastest payback — stop losing cases at the front door.

    Phase 2

    Run the practice

    Build out Mission Control: matters, deadlines, document automation, and the views that replace the MyCase friction.

    Phase 3

    Grow the funnel

    Modernize the OVI/family content and local search so volume rises into a system that can finally hold it.

    Bottom line: a secure, firm-owned system that turns 200 daily calls and 140+ active matters from a daily scramble into something measurable and calm — and an asset Beck Peistrup owns outright. Next step is a scoped plan for Phase 1.
