Phased deliberately: we start with the cheapest, fastest piece with the clearest ROI, prove it, and build out from a system that's already earning its keep.

Stop losing leads at the front door. Stand up automated lead capture and one tracked pipeline so every inbound contact is logged, routed, and followed up — no AI voice agent required to ship.

What ships

  • Missed-call text-back — any unanswered call fires an instant SMS with an intake link

Why it's built differently

Automation a law firm can actually trust

A practice runs on confidentiality and privilege. The viral consumer AI agents weren't built for that — this is. Your data, your keys, full audit trail, human in the loop.

Off-the-shelf AI agents

  • Reach into your files with broad access
  • Pull untrusted third-party "skills" / code
  • Can act on their own — no audit trail
  • Client data flows through someone else's tool
  • You don't own it and can't prove what it did

Your owned system

  • Runs on the firm's own infrastructure (Vercel + Supabase)
  • Row-level isolation & role-based access; 2FA
  • Every action logged — a complete audit trail
  • Your own AI keys; transcribe-then-discard audio by default
  • You own the asset, the data, and the instance

UPL guardrail: automated and AI-suggested messages capture and schedule only — they never give legal advice. An attorney reviews anything substantive.
